03 / ABOUT

Curiosity,
put to work.

Hello, I’m Keyvan.

Exploring the connections between materials, manufacturing, and performance.

My published research brings together additive manufacturing, material characterization, and the behavior of shape-memory alloys. A central thread is understanding how processing choices translate into the structures and properties of NiTi and NiTiHf.

That work extends from crystallographic texture and high-temperature oxidation to the design and evaluation of porous biomedical fixation devices. The publications here reflect collaborative investigations across these connected fields.
